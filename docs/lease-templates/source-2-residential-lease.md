# Source 2 — "Residential Lease Agreement (South Africa)"

Held verbatim as supplied, for reference while the master is reviewed. Not
published and not adoptable.

Structure: a completed **schedule** (items 1.1–1.10), **clauses 2–40**, a
**signature block**, and three annexures — A inspections and inventory, B
utilities and additional arrangements, C optional limited suretyship.

This is the stronger of the two documents and is used as the spine of the master.
It is drafted against the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008, the Rental Housing
Act 50 of 1999, POPIA and PIE, and it already forbids the things that made source
1 risky: no service disconnection to force payment (11.4), no lockouts, seizure of
belongings or service cutoffs as a substitute for legal process (22.3), and no
private seizure under the landlord's hypothec (33.1).

See `master.md` for the merged document and `MERGE-DECISIONS.md` for every point
where the two sources disagreed and which one the master follows.
